## The cycle that never stopped telling stories

On the morning of August 13th, the catchup script was running and generating the same story repeatedly — the logic of "least-posted projects" didn't account for the fact that all of them were already listed. Samuel checked in and said: "Don't post anything about Samuel and Douglas PC". He closed the script, cleaned the publication list and started fresh.

## The phase change that saved the ecosystem

When the `topic_index` reached the end of the `HISTORICAL_TOPICS` file, the script auto-transited from `"phase": "catchup"` to `"phase": "current"`. From that point on, each daily slot suggests a **cyclic update** of the projects, not another new story.

**Real statistics from the workflow:**
- 7 projects in the cycle (`lifelog`, `dogwalk`, `arachne`, `capivara`, `portfolio`, `tatuengine`, `descobertas`)
- 21 stories published during catchup (164-295 words each)
- 3 posts/day → 21 stories = 1 week of publications

## The gate that saved the CI

On September 15th, the `check-post-content.py` gate rejected 18 posts for `E4` (TatuEngine recipe leak). Samuel rejected: "don't expose the recipe". The line of defense: E4 detects `block-traversal`, `mse`, `27 GB`, `114×`, `245 MB` even in hidden posts.

**01/11/2026 adjustment:** E4 now activates for hidden posts too. The post content is saved in the public site **when released** (`hidden: false`). The recipe must be removed from the start.

## Deployment metrics that changed the game

The pipeline now generates:
- 2 separate data structures: `api/ocultos-data.mjs` (hidden posts) and `api/publicos-data.mjs` (released posts)
- Automatic word count verification: `pnpm exec vitest run src/components/PostQuality.test.ts`
- Build: 1177 pages → 1182 pages (WebP optimized covers included)

## The cron that became a tatu

The cron `1ff5884c8cfa` (LifeLog Preview) now triggers 3 times daily: 08:00, 12:00, 16:00. Each execution generates a **hidden** post with `hidden: true`. None goes live automatically. Samuel manually releases in the `/ocultos` panel.

**Release workflow:**
1. Post appears in `/ocultos` with "Release" button
2. Samuel clicks → `PATCH /api/liberar?slug=<slug>`
3. Pipeline auto-redeploys → post becomes public in ~90s

## What comes next

The `lifelog-post-gen.py` now has two modes:
- `mode=catchup`: fills the historical gap
- `mode=current`: updates projects cyclically

When Samuel releases the 13 hidden posts remaining (counted in `LIFELOG_TIMELINE.md`), the system enters `current` mode with posts generated every 4 hours.

## Conclusion

Automation didn't replace human judgment — it amplified it. The pipeline generates structured drafts, Samuel writes the story, and GitHub ensures nothing goes live without review.
